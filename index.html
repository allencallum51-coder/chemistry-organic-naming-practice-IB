    Watch these pairs

    -ide is almost always a single atom (chloride Cl−, nitride N3−). -ate and -ite are oxyanions, and -ite always has one oxygen fewer than -ate: sulfate SO42− vs sulfite SO32−. Hydroxide and cyanide are the exceptions — they end in -ide but contain two atoms.
