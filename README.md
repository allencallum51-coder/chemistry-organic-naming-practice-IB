# IB Chemistry Practice

> **The latest version is on the [`latest`](https://github.com/allencallum51-coder/chemistry-organic-naming-practice-IB/tree/latest) branch.**
>
> This `main` branch only has the first version (organic nomenclature only). The [`ib-chemistry-practice`](https://github.com/allencallum51-coder/chemistry-organic-naming-practice-IB/tree/ib-chemistry-practice) branch has units 01–03.

A practice app for IB Diploma Chemistry (first assessment 2025), in a single web page: revision notes plus auto-marked questions that are generated fresh every time. The latest version has six units:

| Unit | Syllabus | What it covers |
|------|----------|----------------|
| 01 Nomenclature | S3.2 | Stems, functional groups, numbering and the full IUPAC naming algorithm |
| 02 Isomers | S3.2.6, S3.2.7 (HL) | Chain, position and functional group isomers; primary/secondary/tertiary; counting isomers. **HL:** cis-trans and optical isomers |
| 03 Equations & amounts | R2.1, R3.1, R1.3, S1.4 | Balancing, predicting products and salt formulas, moles, concentration, gas volumes, limiting reactant, % yield, % error, atom economy |
| 04 Molecular geometry | S2.2.4, S2.2.6 · HL: S2.2.13–2.2.16 | Electron domains, VSEPR shapes (name them, pick or type), bond angles (lone-pair angles answered as "less than", e.g. <109.5°), molecular polarity. **HL:** five and six electron domains, formal charge, sigma and pi bonds, sp/sp²/sp³ hybridization |
| 05 Equilibrium | R2.3.1–2.3.4 · HL: R2.3.5, R2.3.7 | Dynamic equilibrium, Kc expressions, what the size of K means, K for the reverse reaction, and Le Chatelier drills: does a change in pressure, temperature, concentration or a catalyst shift the equilibrium towards products or reactants, and what happens to the yield and to Kc? **HL:** Q vs K to find the direction, and the sign of ΔG vs K (light on maths) |
| 06 Oxidation states | S3.1.6, R3.2.1 | Assigning oxidation states (including peroxides, hydrides and OF₂), Roman numeral names (iron(III) chloride, sulfate(IV)), oxidised and reduced species, oxidising and reducing agents, redox or not |

It also has an **Ions** section for drilling the ions you need to know, and an **SL / HL switch**: with SL selected, all HL-only notes, question types and test modes are hidden.

## Get the latest version

- **Just the app:** open [`index.html` on the `latest` branch](https://github.com/allencallum51-coder/chemistry-organic-naming-practice-IB/blob/latest/index.html), click **Download raw file**, then double-click the downloaded file to open it in your browser. You don't need to install anything, and it works offline.
- **Whole repo:** use the branch dropdown (top left, currently showing `main`) to switch to `latest`, then click **Code → Download ZIP**.

Your scores, weak areas and SL/HL choice are saved in your browser on that device only.
